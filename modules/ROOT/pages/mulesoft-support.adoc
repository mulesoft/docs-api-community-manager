For professional services customizations, enhancement requests, and other assistance, contact your MuleSoft Customer Success representative.

To report issues or defects in Anypoint API Community Manager, submit a support case through the MuleSoft support portal, available at https://help.mulesoft.com.

The MuleSoft support team works closely with the Salesforce support team to coordinate work on any support tickets involving Salesforce technology.

Service-level agreements (SLAs) for support are available at the MuleSoft https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/legal/Agreements/mulesoft-cloud-offerings-SLA-forSalesforce-uMSA.pdf[Product Support and Maintenance Terms] page.
